## About Me

I am a **Master’s student at the School of Software, Shandong University**, with a primary research focus on **Large Language Models (LLMs)**.

My academic interests include, but are not limited to:

- Large Language Models and their applications  
- Information extraction and named entity recognition  

My research aims to explore how large language models can be effectively adapted to real-world, with an emphasis on robustness, controllability, and structured output generation.
